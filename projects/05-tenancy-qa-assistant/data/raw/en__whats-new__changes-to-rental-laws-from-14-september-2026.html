Changes to rental laws from 14 September 2026

14 September 2026

News

Do you rent? From 14 September 2026, certain rental rules are changing.

The Residential Tenancies Board (RTB) can fine your landlord for breaking certain rental rules, including:

  • Not registering a tenancy
  • Not updating details about a tenancy
  • Not giving rent setting information at the start of a tenancy
  • Setting the rent above the rent increase limits
  • Not notifying the RTB of a rent exemption
  • Not providing a rent review notice correctly

Your landlord can be fined €200 for breaking the rent increase rules and €100 for breaking the registration rules.

Your landlord can no longer send you a notice of termination by post. Instead, they must email, hand-deliver, or leave it at your address.

Your landlord now has 7 days from when you get a notice of termination or a notice of rent review to give a copy of it to the RTB. Previously, they had to send a copy at the same time.

The RTB must now hold adjudication hearings in public and publish adjudicator's reports on its website.

There are also changes to appeal and hearing notice times for some RTB dispute cases.

  • The process for reviewing rent
  • Notice of termination if your landlord wants you to leave

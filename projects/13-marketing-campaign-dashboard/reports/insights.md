## Hold-out test (paid social prospecting)

10% of people were randomly withheld. Among people eligible for prospecting, conversion was 17.02% exposed vs 13.58% held out (lift 3.44 pp, 95% CI 2.47 to 4.41 pp, p = 3.2e-12). Estimated incremental conversions in the exposed group: 1,685 (95% CI 1,211–2,159); simulated truth 1,966.

## Caveats

Simulated data with a known answer. Real attribution cannot be scored this way; that is why the recommendation is to run hold-out tests on the biggest budget lines.

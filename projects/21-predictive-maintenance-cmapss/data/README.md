# data/

Nothing is committed here. `src/data.py` downloads the NASA C-MAPSS archive
(12.4 MB) into `downloads/`, checks its SHA-256
(`c9c5dec1…12ad3b2`) and extracts `CMAPSSData/`. Both folders are
git-ignored. The data is a US Government work in the public domain; cite
Saxena et al. (2008), PHM08.
